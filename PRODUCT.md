# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Vietnamese speakers who study English independently, especially learners preparing for the VSTEP exam. They use Lingua regularly to study vocabulary and grammar, practice skills, and track progress.

## Product Purpose

Lingua is a learning hub for self-directed English study. It supports daily learning and exam preparation through study modules and progress tracking.

## Positioning

The product's differentiating mechanism is not yet established.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing learning features, data, and behavior.
- Keep the Lingua brand.
- Support both desktop web and mobile web.
- Do not change business logic as part of visual work.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains vocabulary, grammar, skills, VSTEP, writing, progress, planning, and mind-map modules. No user-approved differentiating claims or testimonials have been provided; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make daily learning easy to understand and resume.
- Keep study content and progress clear and scannable.
- Preserve user data and existing learning workflows.
- Support learners on both desktop and mobile web.
