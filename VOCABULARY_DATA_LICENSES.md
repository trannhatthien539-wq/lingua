# Vocabulary data sources and licenses

The three 4,000-word decks in `src/data/essentialWordsPart1.js`,
`src/data/essentialWordsPart2.js`, and `src/data/essentialWordsPart3.js` combine:

- The first 1,000 entries from this project's existing common-word dataset.
- Additional English lemmas and fallback English glosses from WordNet 3.0.
- Vietnamese translations extracted from English Wiktionary entries where available.
